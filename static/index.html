Frequently asked questions

How can I make a video file smaller?

Choose a lower available resolution, try H.265 if your device supports it, or enable Compact encoding. Smaller files can take longer to process, and compression may affect visual quality.

Why might a video link fail?

Some links may be private, restricted, expired, or unsupported. Use a link you can access and have permission to save. Not every platform or video is guaranteed to work.

What is the difference between H.264 and H.265?

H.264 is widely supported by devices and apps. H.265 can often deliver a smaller file at similar visual quality, but compatibility varies by device and software.

Can I download any video?

No. Only download videos you own or have permission to save, and follow the source platform’s terms and applicable copyright laws.
